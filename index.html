You Don't Have to Earn My Belief in You

“I think this is my favourite part.”

“the part where something is still becoming.”

before everyone else can see what it is.

before there's a finished version.

before there's a result to point at.

you already started building it.

“and yk what, Ammu?”

“I don't need the finished version to believe in you.”

I don't need to wait for the company to become huge.

I don't need to wait for the next milestone.

I don't need a list of achievements.

I don't need proof.

“I've already seen you.”

“I'VE SEEN YOU BUILD”

PART 01 — THE IDEA

I've seen you take an idea seriously enough to actually do something with it.

PART 02 — THE FIGURING IT OUT

I've seen you figure things out as you go.

Not because everything was handed to you with instructions.

but because apparently your brain's favourite response to a problem is just... “okay, let's see.” 😂

PART 03 — THE RIDICULOUS AMOUNT OF THINGS

I've watched you somehow have five things going on at once and still casually decide there's probably room for one more.

“bro, respectfully... HOW.” 😂

PART 04 — THE STARTING FROM SCRATCH

And now you're building something that's yours.

Something that didn't exist in this form before you decided to make it exist.

“that's a pretty incredible thing.”

“And that's why I don't need to know how the story ends.”

I don't know exactly what this company will become.

I don't know how many times you'll change direction.

I don't know which ideas will become the big ones.

I don't know what the next few years will look like.

“but I know who is building it.”

and honestly? that's enough for me.

I'm not here to be your business consultant.

you already have enough people for that. 😂

I'm just your Kanmani.

the annoying person in the corner who gets ridiculously happy when you tell me about something you're building...

who will listen to your explanations even when I understand approximately 37% of what you're talking about...

and who will still look at you and think,

“yeah. that's my Ammu.” 💜

“you don't have to earn my belief in you.”

You already have it.

before the next win.

before the next project.

before the next stage.

before anyone else gets to see what you're building.

“I've already got your back.”

Ammu,

go build your thing.

Make it messy.

Make it ambitious.

Change your mind.

Try something ridiculous.

figure things out.

learn as you go.

make mistakes.

build it again.

whatever the road looks like, I'll be here somewhere in your corner, ridiculously proud that you decided to take the road in the first place.

I don't need the ending to believe in the beginning.

I already believe in you.

Now go make that dream annoyingly real. 😂💜

— your Kanmani
